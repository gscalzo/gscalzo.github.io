## Follow-up on Giordano’s notes

- **WebDeLDN / server-side Swift:** event and 2016 year supported by the older CV; title matched by Giordano’s note. Added publicly as 2016. November is a recollection, not independently verified.
- **Swift London / Flappy Bird:** title matched by Giordano’s note and the [books page](https://gioscalzo.com/books/); CV confirms the 2014 appearance. Added publicly as 2014. July remains approximate.
- **XP-It London / Swift introduction:** event and year supported by the CV; title matched by Giordano’s note. Added publicly as 2014, using the CV’s event name. June remains approximate.
- **Better Software:** the [XPUG Milano archive](http://milano-xpug.pbworks.com/) explicitly says meeting 90 on **26 May 2010** repeated Better Software 2010 presentations, and links Giordano’s **Better Software Developers**, deck 3989933. [MokaByte](https://mokabyte.it/2010/05/01/bettersoftware2010/) confirms the Florence conference dates **5–6 May 2010**; these are conference dates, not an exact session day. Both appearances added. The 2011-uploaded *Better Software: introduction to good code* deck remains unassigned.
- **WhyMCA:** Giordano identifies *TDD iPhone for Dummies* as this talk. An [organiser’s announcement](https://www.androidiani.com/applicazioni/whymca-2010-sviluppo-mobile-e-molto-altro-ancora-6230) confirms **21 May 2010**, Politecnico di Milano, Bovisa. Added with that date.
- **Agile iPhone Development:** a [March 2010 mailing-list thread](https://groups.google.com/g/milano-codingdojo/c/9zOMtbJtj4Q) contains Giordano’s proposal to repeat his AgileCamp introduction, and his confirmation of the XPUG dojo on **31 March 2010** at WebScience. Luca Marrocco explicitly calls the earlier event **AgileCamp 2010**. Giordano subsequently confirmed that it was a barcamp in **Lugano, Switzerland, on the Saturday after 22 February 2010**, which is **27 February 2010**. This supersedes his tentative 2009 recollection above; the precise date and location are confirmed by the speaker, rather than independently sourced. The XPUG archive links the exact deck under meeting 86 but repeats the January date from meeting 82; the dated email resolves that apparent copy-and-paste error. Added both appearances and their dates publicly. The [participant report from 17 January 2009](https://blog.nicolamattina.it/2009/01/agilecamp-a-lugano/) concerns a different edition.
- **KataYahtzee:** XPUG meeting 82 confirms **27 January 2010**, organised by Giordano at Sourcesense. Added.
- **BDD:** XPUG meeting 79 confirms **2 December 2009**, at Sourcesense, with the programme title *Behaviour-Driven Development: TDD to infinity and beyond*. Added, retaining the deck title in the archive.
- **Scrum in an hour:** internal training is still tentative. Kept the deck, with no event attribution.

Additional leads found in the XPUG archive: Giordano performed the String Evaluator Kata in Scala at meeting 80 (listed as 13 December 2009, [recording](https://vimeo.com/8326880)); he also devised and worked through RatingKata with Raffaele Salmaso and Andrea Francia on 1 February 2012. These are coding sessions, not yet added as separate talks; the old video’s availability has not been checked.

- The collection includes 14 technical/leadership decks plus [10 minutes of me: Giordano Scalzo's Visual Resume](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/10-minutes-of-me-giordano-scalzos-visual-resume/1936881), uploaded 1 September 2009. The visual CV is not counted as a talk.

These are grouped under one title, not counted as separate appearances.
- CouchApps is an additional co-presented deck hosted by Federico Galassi. Its [Vimeo record](https://vimeo.com/21615605) confirms the title, both speakers and NoSQL Day tags, but currently says the video is unavailable. The public talks page links the slides instead.
- Search results show a GrUSP Vimeo recording of JavaScript Survival Guide in related-video lists, but its own working URL was not verified. No guessed video link was added.
- Londroid was incorrectly labelled June on the site. The organiser's [announcement](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/londroid_londroid-newday-thu-may-29-2025-600-activity-7330230157372198913-RtQV) and Meetup confirm 29 May 2025; Novoda uploaded the video on 6 June 2025. - WebTech's Joind.in record says 9 November 2010; the matching deck says 10 November. The public page uses November 2010 until the exact delivery day is confirmed.
- Avanscoperta's [past courses index](https://www.avanscoperta.it/it/i-corsi-passati/) attributes Professional iOS Development to Giordano, but the [detail page](https://www.avanscoperta.it/it/training/professional-ios-development/) refers to Andrea and identifies Andrea Francia in metadata. Kept out of the public list pending clarification.
- Attendance, likes, recommendations and other speakers' talks were not counted as Giordano's appearances.
