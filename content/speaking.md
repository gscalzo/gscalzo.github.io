I speak about AI engineering, agentic coding, and growing teams. My talks draw on experiments with real software, including the parts that went wrong.

## NSLondon, November 2025

**AI-Accelerated Mobile Development: From idea to build, faster than ever**

At Google London on 26 November 2025, I demonstrated a macOS QR-code app and an iOS bill splitter. The session covers how agents work, how to give them useful context, and why planning and testing matter as more implementation is delegated.
