I speak about AI engineering, agentic coding, and growing teams. My talks draw on experiments with real software, including the parts that went wrong.

## Upcoming talks

### LDX3 New York, 15–16 September 2026

**Tackling the junior developer crisis in the age of AI**

How do we help junior engineers build judgement when AI can produce convincing code before they understand it? I'll share mentoring exercises that develop debugging, explanation, and critical thinking while keeping AI in the workflow.

[Conference agenda and session details](https://leaddev.com/leaddev-new-york/agenda/)

### agentic codingCon, Berlin, 7–9 October 2026

**Just One More Prompt: Why Agentic Coding Hooks Us** · Keynote

The feature is almost done, so you ask for one more thing. Then another. This keynote explores why that loop is so hard to leave, how to recognise when flow becomes compulsion, and practical stopping rules for developers and teams.

[Talk and speaker details](https://www.nextappcon.com/agentic-codingcon-new-speakers/gio-scalzo) · [Conference](https://www.nextappcon.com/agentic-coding-con)

## Recent recordings

### NSLondon, November 2025

**AI-Accelerated Mobile Development: From idea to build, faster than ever**

At Google London on 26 November 2025, I demonstrated a macOS QR-code app and an iOS bill splitter. The session covers how agents work, how to give them useful context, and why planning and testing matter as more implementation is delegated.

[Read the blog post](/blog/ai-accelerated-mobile-development-nslondon/) · [Watch the video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QqHiv3suI6E) · [NSLondon event](https://www.meetup.com/nslondon/events/311356134/)

### Londroid, May 2025

**AI-Accelerated Mobile Development**
