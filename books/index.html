It began with a Swift demo at NSLondon and one thought: “Let’s give it a try, at least for a chapter.” Six published books later, I’m still giving it a try.

I wrote my first book after converting a Flappy Bird clone to Swift on the night Apple announced the language. Writing became another way to learn in public. The subjects have changed, from Swift and software design to engineering in the age of AI, but that part has not.
